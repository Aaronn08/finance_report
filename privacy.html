Personal Finance Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Personal Finance Tracker ("the application") accesses, processes, and stores information when used by its owner for personal financial organization.

1. Information accessed

With the account owner's authorization, the application may access Gmail messages permitted by the configured Google OAuth scopes. It is intended to process relevant financial notifications, such as transaction confirmations, payment receipts, and transfer notifications.

Depending on the information contained in a message, extracted fields may include transaction date, amount, currency, merchant, transaction type, payment method, account details, reference number, and a description.

2. How information is used

Information is used to:

  • Identify relevant financial transaction notifications.
  • Extract and categorize transaction details.
  • Prevent duplicate transaction records where configured.
  • Maintain personal financial records and monthly summaries.

Gmail information is not intended to be used for advertising, selling personal information, or profiling other people.

3. Processing and storage

The application uses a self-hosted n8n workflow running on the owner's computer. Where configured, email text is processed by a locally running Ollama language model. Local processing depends on the actual workflow configuration and does not mean every service involved operates locally.

Extracted transaction records may be stored in Google Sheets. Gmail messages remain subject to Google's own storage and retention practices. Data may also be temporarily present in workflow execution data or logs, depending on the n8n settings.

4. Sharing and third-party services

The application owner does not intend to sell or rent personal financial information. Information may be processed by services necessary for the application's operation, including Google Gmail, Google Sheets, and any other service explicitly configured in the workflow.

Google services process data under Google's applicable terms and privacy policies. If the workflow is changed to send email content or transaction details to another external service, this policy must be updated to reflect that processing.

5. Data retention and deletion

Transaction records may remain in Google Sheets until the owner deletes them. The owner can delete stored records, clear applicable workflow execution data, and remove any other copies maintained by the application.

Access to Gmail can be revoked through the Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revoking access prevents future access through that authorization but does not automatically delete previously stored records.

6. Security

The application is designed for personal use. The owner should protect the computer running n8n, restrict access to credentials, use appropriate account security, and avoid publishing workflow exports, tokens, passwords, or financial records.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's privacy

This personal finance application is not designed to collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality, data handling, or connected services change. The latest version will be published on this page.
