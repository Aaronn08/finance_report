Personal Finance Tracker
Personal-use project

Understand your personal finances in one place.

Personal Finance Tracker is a personal automation project designed to organize financial transaction notifications and help maintain a record of income, expenses, transfers, and cash transactions.

The project uses n8n to automate workflows, with locally run language models where configured, and Google Sheets to organize transaction records and monthly summaries.

What the application does

  • Processes relevant financial transaction emails from Gmail.
  • Extracts transaction details such as date, amount, merchant, and payment method.
  • Organizes transaction records in Google Sheets.
  • Supports personal expense tracking and monthly summaries.

Privacy and data protection

This project is intended for personal financial organization. Gmail data is processed only for the functions described in the Privacy Policy. Access to Gmail is subject to the permissions granted by the account owner.
